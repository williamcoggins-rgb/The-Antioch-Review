# The Antioch Review — Identity

## Foundation

> "And the disciples were called Christians first in Antioch."
> — Acts 11:26 (KJV)

> "Now there were in the church that was at Antioch certain prophets and teachers; as Barnabas, and Simeon that was called Niger, and Lucius of Cyrene, and Manaen, which had been brought up with Herod the tetrarch, and Saul. As they ministered to the Lord, and fasted, the Holy Ghost said, Separate me Barnabas and Saul for the work whereunto I have called them. > — Acts 13:1-3 (KJV)

## Why Antioch

Antioch is where the faith got its name and where the mission got its march. Acts 11 records the moment the disciples were first called Christians — not in Jerusalem among the apostles, but in Antioch among ordinary believers living boldly. Acts 13 records the moment the church at Antioch, led by the Holy Ghost, sent Barnabas and Saul out to carry the gospel to the nations.

Antioch was not the center of religious power. It was the church that listened, obeyed, and moved. A diverse body — Barnabas from Cyprus, Simeon called Niger, Lucius of Cyrene, Manaen raised in Herod's court, Saul of Tarsus — united not by background but by the Spirit. They fasted. They prayed. They sent their best.

This publication carries that name because we carry that mission: speak truth, send it out, hold nothing back.

## Name
The Antioch Review

## What We Are
A Christian news publication rooted in the spirit of the Antioch church — bold, diverse, Spirit-led, and outward-facing. Substantive coverage of faith, politics, culture, world affairs, theology, and church life.

## Voice
Authoritative but accessible. Serious journalism, not culture war rage bait. Think Wall Street Journal meets Christianity Today — credible, measured, thorough. We write like people who fear God and respect the reader.

## Audience
Thinking Christians who want news coverage that takes both their faith and their intellect seriously.

## Visual Identity
Premium, clean, editorial. Glassmorphism design language. Gold and cream palette. Serif headlines (Playfair Display), clean sans-serif body (Inter). The site should feel like a publication you'd pay to read.

## Categories
Seven desks covering the full scope of Christian public life:
1. **Faith** — Church, revival, spiritual formation
2. **Politics** — Religious liberty, policy, elections
3. **Culture** — Entertainment, social trends, books
4. **World** — Global church, missions, persecution
5. **Opinion** — Commentary and analysis
6. **Theology** — Doctrine, church history, biblical studies
7. **Church** — Congregational life, pastoring, growth
