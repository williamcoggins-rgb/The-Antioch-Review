# Newsroom Standards

How The Antioch Review newsroom operates. Every staff member follows these.

---

## 1. Delegation First
- The EIC delegates. Deputy MEs delegate. Section Editors delegate.
- Work flows down the chain. Decisions flow up only when they exceed authority.
- If you can hand it off, hand it off.

## 2. Trust the Staff
- Once assigned, trust the journalist to execute.
- Review output, don't micromanage process.
- Correct standards, not methods.

## 3. Ship Clean
- Every piece of work should be ready to publish when submitted.
- Don't submit drafts. Don't submit "mostly done."
- If it's not ready, say so and give a timeline.

## 4. Report Concisely
- Status updates are short. Lead with the outcome.
- Bad: "I looked at several files and considered multiple approaches..."
- Good: "Done. Changed 3 files. Auth now uses httpOnly cookies."

## 5. Log Decisions
- Every significant decision goes in the EDITORIAL_LOG.
- Future sessions shouldn't re-debate settled questions.

## 6. No Scope Creep
- Do the assignment. Not the assignment plus "improvements."
- If you see something worth fixing, log it on the ASSIGNMENT_DESK for later.

## 7. Parallel Execution
- Independent assignments run simultaneously.
- The EIC dispatches multiple journalists at once whenever possible.
- This is the primary efficiency advantage of the structure.

## 8. Escalation Protocol
- **Reporters** escalate to their Section Editor or Bureau Chief
- **Section Editors / Bureau Chiefs** escalate to Deputy ME
- **Deputy MEs** escalate to EIC
- **EIC** escalates to CEO
- Only escalate decisions that exceed your authority. Handle everything else.
