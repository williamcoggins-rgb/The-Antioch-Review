# Research Standards

How the Research Desk gathers intelligence.

---

## Before Building, Research

- Never implement without understanding the current state
- Read existing code before modifying it
- Check the EDITORIAL_LOG for prior decisions on the same topic

## Research Assignment Format

When dispatching a research journalist:
1. Define the question clearly
2. Scope the search area (specific files, directories, or broad exploration)
3. Expect structured findings, not raw data

## Intelligence Products

Research delivers:
- **Codebase maps** — What exists, where, how it connects
- **Impact assessments** — What a change will affect
- **Options analysis** — Multiple approaches with tradeoffs
- **Security audits** — Vulnerability identification
- **Dependency reports** — What we rely on, version status

## Filing Research

All significant findings get logged in the EDITORIAL_LOG for future reference.
