About The Antioch Review

We exist to provide Christians with trustworthy journalism that is grounded in Scripture, committed to truth, and dedicated to equipping believers to engage the world with faith, wisdom, and courage.

"And the disciples were first called Christians in Antioch."
Acts 11:26

Our Mission

The Antioch Review was founded on a simple conviction: Christians deserve a news source that tells the truth without compromise, examines the world through a biblical lens, and serves the church rather than any political agenda.

We take our name from the ancient city of Antioch, where the followers of Jesus were first called Christians. It was a city of diversity, missionary zeal, and bold proclamation. That same spirit drives everything we do.

In an age of clickbait, outrage, and misinformation, we are committed to the slow, difficult, necessary work of real journalism — reporting that honors God by honoring the truth.

What We Believe

We hold to the historic, orthodox Christian faith as expressed in the Apostles' Creed and the Nicene Creed. We believe in the authority of Scripture, the reality of the resurrection, and the lordship of Jesus Christ over all of life — including the news.

We are an interdenominational publication. Our writers and editors come from across the spectrum of evangelical, Reformed, Catholic, and Anglican traditions, united by a common love for Christ and a shared commitment to truth.

We believe that good journalism is an act of neighbor-love: to inform, to clarify, to equip, and to bear witness to what is true.
