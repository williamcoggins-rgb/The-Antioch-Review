Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?

Yes. All subscriptions are month-to-month unless you choose an annual plan. You can cancel at any time from your account settings, and you'll continue to have access through the end of your billing period.

Do you offer student or ministry discounts?

Yes. We offer 50% off for seminary students and active ministry workers. Contact us at subscriptions@theantiochreview.com with verification of your status.

Can I gift a subscription?

Patron-tier subscribers can gift one free subscription per year. All tiers can purchase gift subscriptions at any time through our gift page.

How is The Antioch Review funded?

We are primarily funded by our subscribers. We accept limited advertising that aligns with our values, but subscriber support is the backbone of our operation. This keeps our journalism independent and accountable to you, our readers.

What denominations does The Antioch Review represent?

We are an interdenominational publication rooted in historic, orthodox Christianity. Our team and contributors come from a variety of Protestant and Catholic traditions, united by a commitment to the authority of Scripture and the centrality of Christ.
